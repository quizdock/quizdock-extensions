# My QuizDock quizzes and themes

Quizzes and quiz themes made with [QuizDock](https://github.com/quizdock/quizdock), shared through the QuizDock community store.

## Publish a quiz

1. In QuizDock, open the quiz and choose **Export for publication**. You get a `.quizdock.zip` file.
2. Unzip it on your computer: you get a folder.
3. Here, open the **quizzes** folder, click **Add file → Upload files**, drag the whole folder onto the page, then **Commit changes**.

To update a quiz, upload its new folder over the old one (delete the old folder if its name differs). To remove it, delete its folder. The store builds the zips itself: never upload a zip.

## Publish a theme

1. In QuizDock, open **Themes**, open the theme and choose **Export**. You get a `.quizdock-theme.zip` file.
2. Unzip it: you get a folder.
3. Here, open the **themes** folder (create it with **Add file → Upload files** the first time), drag the whole folder onto the page, then **Commit changes**.

Update and remove a theme as a quiz.

The full guide: [publish your quizzes and themes](https://github.com/quizdock/quizdock-store/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
